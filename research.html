RESEARCH

Research Experience

My research experience spans ultrafast 2D-IR spectroscopy, supramolecular chemistry, biomolecular condensates, solvation shell spectroscopy, all-atom molecular dynamics, and quantum computation for molecular energies, carried out at IISER Kolkata, CSIR-NCL Pune and Ruhr-University Bochum.

01

August 2026 – Present · On-site

Master’s Thesis · IISER Kolkata & CSIR-NCL Pune, India

Chiral Supramolecular Assembly and Stereoselective Catalysis Regulated by Water Dynamics in Liquid Condensates

Supervisor: Dr. Debangshu Chaudhuri & Dr. Sayan Bagchi (Co-supervisor)

I synthesise and characterise amino acid functionalised perylene diimide dye molecules, and investigate the structural and spectroscopic properties of their supramolecular assemblies in molecular condensates using UV-Visible and circular dichroism spectroscopy together with microscopic techniques.

In parallel, I study ultrafast water dynamics inside the condensates using 2D-IR spectroscopy on isotopically labelled water.

02

May – July 2026 · On-site

Visiting Research Intern · Ruhr-University Bochum, Germany

Solvation Shell Spectroscopy of Solutes Using UV-Vis Spectrophotometer

Supervisor: Prof. Dr. Poul B. Petersen

Carried out UV-Visible spectroscopy experiments to investigate how co-solutes alter the solvation shell of a primary solute. Different co-solutes (NaN3, NaNO3 and NaSCN) were examined systematically while tert-butanol was kept as the primary solute.

Also assisted with sum frequency generation (SFG) spectroscopy experiments on air–water interfaces.

03

August – December 2025 · On-site

Semester Project Student · IISER Kolkata, India

Calculation of Preferential Interaction Coefficient of Salt Around a Polyelectrolyte

Supervisor: Dr. Susmita Roy

Simulated all-atom molecular dynamics of salt systems (NaCl, KCl and MgCl2) surrounding a 12-base-pair DNA polyelectrolyte, and developed custom Python scripts to process the trajectory data and compute preferential interaction coefficients.

Rank-ordering the salts by their computed coefficients reconstructed the Hofmeister series.

May – July 2025 · On-site

Summer Research Intern · CSIR-NCL, Pune, India

2D-IR Spectroscopy on Biomolecular Condensates

Supervisor: Dr. Sayan Bagchi

Used UV-Visible and FTIR spectroscopy to characterise liquid–liquid phase separation (LLPS) in bovine serum albumin (BSA), and operated an ultrafast 2D-IR spectroscopy setup to explore the phase-specific dynamics of the dense and dilute phases of BSA condensates.

Processed and analysed the 2D-IR spectral datasets with custom MATLAB pipelines.

Error Mitigation in Quantum Computation
