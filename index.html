CURRENT RESEARCH

MASTER'S THESIS · IISER KOLKATA & CSIR-NCL PUNE

Chiral assembly and catalysis, regulated by water dynamics.

My thesis centres on amino acid functionalised perylene diimide dyes and the chiral supramolecular assemblies they form inside liquid condensates. I synthesise and characterise the dyes, then study the structure and spectroscopy of the assemblies using UV-Visible and circular dichroism spectroscopy together with microscopy.

A second strand of the work uses 2D-IR spectroscopy on isotopically labelled water to follow ultrafast water dynamics inside the condensates, and asks how that dynamics regulates chirality and stereoselective catalysis.
